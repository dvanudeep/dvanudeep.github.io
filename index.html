About

I am an AI Security & Governance consultant specializing in helping enterprises adopt Artificial Intelligence securely, responsibly, and at scale.

Over the past 6+ years, I have worked across Deloitte, Accenture, and TCS delivering AI governance programs, secure-by-design initiatives, enterprise cyber risk assessments, and cloud security strategies for large organizations.

My expertise spans the complete AI lifecycle: AI risk identification, governance framework design, security architecture reviews, model risk management, Responsible AI implementation, and regulatory alignment with NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, ISO 27001, EU AI Act, and NIST 800-53.

I enjoy translating complex AI and cloud security risks into practical engineering controls, audit-ready evidence, and executive-level recommendations that help organizations innovate safely.
